About North Star

Business with a bigger purpose.

North Star BAS Marketplace Ltd. was established in Alberta in 2026 with a simple idea: build healthy, sustainable ventures that create real value while keeping the purpose behind the business clear.

We look for opportunities where thoughtful buying, improving, building, selling, or holding can create value over time. That includes real estate and marketplace opportunities today, with room to pursue other practical ventures as the company grows.

Our focus is not short-term hype. We want to make disciplined decisions, protect what has been entrusted to us, reinvest wisely, and build something that can serve people and make an impact for years to come.

How we operate

Three principles guide the way we build.

01

Integrity

Do what is right, communicate clearly, and build trust into every decision, transaction, and relationship.

02

Stewardship

Treat capital, property, opportunity, and influence as things to be managed carefully rather than consumed carelessly.

03

Excellence

Pursue quality, thoughtful execution, and continual improvement in the way opportunities are evaluated and developed.

Our approach

Grow carefully. Reinvest intentionally.

North Star is built around long-term thinking. We believe healthy growth comes from disciplined purchases, conservative planning, responsible use of capital, and the willingness to walk away from opportunities that do not make sense.

Profits are viewed as fuel for future growth and future impact. The goal is to strengthen the company over time, expand its ability to create value, and keep building from a solid foundation rather than chasing growth for its own sake.

The long view

Success is not only measured by what the business owns, but by what the business is ultimately able to accomplish.

We want North Star to become a durable, well-run company that creates opportunity, builds wealth responsibly, and turns business success into meaningful impact beyond itself.
