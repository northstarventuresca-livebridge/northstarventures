About North Star

More than one company. One direction.

North Star Ventures is the broader venture platform behind a growing collection of businesses and opportunities. Rather than being defined by one industry, our mandate is to identify strong situations, place the right structure around them, and build long-term value through ownership, improvement, partnership, and disciplined execution.

Our interests can range from established businesses and succession situations to real estate, technology, AI, equipment, inventory, strategic assets, and unusual opportunities that do not fit neatly into a traditional category.

The common thread is simple: we want to own and build things that are useful, sustainable, well stewarded, and capable of producing meaningful long-term impact.

What we pursue

A broad mandate for practical, high-value opportunities.

01 · ACQUISITIONS

Business Acquisitions & Succession

Established businesses, owner transitions, succession situations, and companies where stronger stewardship, modernization, operational support, or a new ownership chapter can create value.

02 · REAL ESTATE

Property & Real Estate Ventures

Residential and commercial acquisitions, value-add property, redevelopment, rentals, strategic land or buildings, and larger transactions where disciplined ownership can improve the outcome.

03 · TECHNOLOGY

AI & Technology Ventures

Practical AI, software, automation, and technology businesses that solve real problems, create recurring value, or can become durable operating companies.

04 · ASSETS

Strategic Assets & Large Purchases

Equipment, inventory, bulk acquisitions, underused assets, distressed or special situations, and large marketplace opportunities where intelligent repositioning can unlock value.

05 · PARTNERSHIPS

Founder & Operator Partnerships

Working alongside capable founders, operators, owners, or property partners when aligned values and complementary strengths can create something stronger together.

06 · NEW VENTURES

Build From the Ground Up

New businesses and operating companies developed internally when we see a real market need, a strong model, and an opportunity worth building for the long term.
