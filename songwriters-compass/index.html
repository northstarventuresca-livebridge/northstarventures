A future songwriting toolkit designed to help you create with intention.

01 / CAPTURE

Journal anywhere

Write down a prayer, melody idea, lyric or thought the moment it comes — at home, church or on the go.

02 / ORGANIZE

Your song library

Keep song ideas, works in progress and completed lyrics in one personal, organized place.

03 / DEVELOP

AI as a companion

Get thoughtful questions and songwriting suggestions that help develop your own words rather than replacing them.

04 / REFLECT

Scripture & notes

Keep the Bible passages, experiences and prayers behind each song alongside your lyrics.

05 / RETURN

Pick up where you left off

Come back to unfinished songs, revisit old ideas and keep growing them over time.

06 / SHARE

Save & export

Bring your finished songs into rehearsals, worship teams and wherever God leads them next.

The vision in three simple steps

Listen. Write. Grow.

01

Start with inspiration.

Spend time with God. Capture the scripture, thought or moment stirring in your heart.

02

Write in your own voice.

Develop your lyrics and ideas. Let the companion help with questions, clarity and structure when you want it.

03

Keep building your song.

Save your progress, come back when you're ready and share the finished work with others.
